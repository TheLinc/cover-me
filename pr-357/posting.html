Warehouse Supervisor

Northwind Traders is hiring a Warehouse Supervisor to lead the night shift at our Riverside depot. You will plan the shift, train new staff and keep the floor safe.

What you will do

  • Plan picking and loading for a team of twelve.
  • Train new staff on forklifts and safety checks.
  • Report stock counts to the depot manager each week.

About the depot

The Riverside depot ships groceries and household goods to forty stores across the region. The night shift runs from 10 pm to 6 am, five nights a week, with a shift allowance and paid overtime. You will report to the depot manager and work with the day supervisor on handovers.

What you bring

  • Two years leading a warehouse team.
  • A forklift licence.
