Senior Python Developer New
Eliassen Group Denver, US

Job Title

Senior Python Developer

Job Description

This is a remote Senior Python Developer role building and operating components of a SaaS risk and compliance AI platform, with work spanning data ingestion, storage, search, cloud deployment, modernization, and production support.

Responsibilities:

  • Design, develop, and maintain Python services and data pipelines on Linux.
  • Ingest, normalize, and query large, messy datasets.
  • Implement and optimize data models in PostgreSQL or similar relational databases.
  • Build and tune search functionality with Elasticsearch or comparable tools.
  • Deploy and operate AWS workloads across services including Lambda, ECS, EC2, RDS, SQS, S3, Route 53, and core networking.
  • Manage components end to end, including code, testing and validation, dependencies, and deployment strategies.
  • Help modernize the screening platform while maintaining production stability.

Restrictions

  • No telecommuting
  • No Agencies Please

Requirements

Must:

  • Willing and able to work on a W-2 basis.
  • At least 5 years of industry experience.
  • Strong experience with Python and Linux.
  • Proven ability to ingest, store, and query messy, large-scale data.
  • Experience with PostgreSQL or another SQL relational database.
  • Familiarity with Elasticsearch or another NoSQL search engine.
  • Hands-on AWS experience with Lambda, ECS, EC2, RDS, SQS, S3, Route 53, and networking concepts.
  • Initiative in independent or self-managed projects; adaptable, self-motivated, and curious, with startup experience preferred.
  • Nice to have: experience with NER, NLP, modeling, machine learning, or agent frameworks such as LangGraph.

About the Company

Eligible consultants are offered medical, dental, and vision coverage, a 401(k) plan with company matching, life insurance, and additional benefits. Eliassen Group is a strategic consulting firm that serves clients through technology, business advisory, and life sciences solutions. AI tools are used during the initial application screening process, with AI-assisted evaluations and responses reviewed by human recruiters. Eliassen Group is an equal opportunity and affirmative action employer committed to fostering an inclusive workplace.

Contact Info

  • E-mail contact: dontsendemail@usetheapply.link
