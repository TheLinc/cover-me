Job post

At Contoso, we build accounting software for small farms and the co-ops that buy from them.

Our invoicing product handles more than two million invoices a year. We're hiring a Backend Developer to help it grow into payments and payroll.

What You'll Do

  • Design and build APIs for invoicing, payments and payroll.
  • Own services end to end, from the first design document to on-call.
  • Improve the performance of our PostgreSQL queries and background jobs.
  • Review code and pair with teammates across three time zones.

What We Look For

  • 2+ years building web services in Python, Go or Kotlin.
  • Experience with relational databases and writing SQL by hand.
  • Comfort with testing, code review and continuous delivery.

Pay Grade - Canada
CAD $95,000 - $120,000 a year, depending on location and experience.

Remote-first
Contoso is a remote-first company. Most roles can be done from anywhere in the country of employment.

We welcome applicants of every background and will provide accommodations during the interview process on request.

Contoso does not accept unsolicited resumes from recruiting agencies.
