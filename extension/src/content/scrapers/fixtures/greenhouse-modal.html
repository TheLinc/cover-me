Platform Engineer

Fabrikam

RemoteCanada

Posted · 4 days ago

ABOUT FABRIKAM

Fabrikam makes scheduling software for regional bus and ferry operators. Our tools plan routes, assign drivers and crews, and tell riders when the next departure leaves.

More than 140 transit agencies run their daily service on Fabrikam, from small island ferries to city bus networks with a thousand vehicles.

ABOUT THE ROLE

We're hiring a Platform Engineer to look after the systems every Fabrikam product runs on: our build pipeline, our Kubernetes clusters, and the shared services that handle sign-in, billing and notifications.
You'll join a team of five and report to the Head of Infrastructure.

WHAT YOU'LL DO

  • Run and improve our Kubernetes clusters across two cloud regions.
  • Own the build pipeline so every team can ship a change in under fifteen minutes.
  • Write Terraform for new services and keep existing modules tidy.
  • Set up monitoring and alerts that tell us about problems before agencies do.
  • Take part in a shared on-call rotation, one week in six.
  • Help product teams design services that are cheap to run and easy to debug.

WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR

  • 4+ years running production systems in a cloud environment.
  • Hands-on experience with Kubernetes and Terraform.
  • Comfort writing Go or Python for tools and automation.
  • Experience with PostgreSQL in production, including backups and upgrades.
  • Clear written communication; most of our planning happens in documents.

NICE TO HAVE

  • Experience in transport, logistics or another 24/7 operation.
  • Familiarity with OpenTelemetry.

BENEFITS

We pay for the things that make remote work work. That includes a home office budget, a yearly learning allowance, and four weeks of paid vacation from your first year.

COMPENSATION

The salary range for this position is $120,000–$150,000 CAD. The final offer depends on experience and location.

EQUAL OPPORTUNITY

Fabrikam is an equal opportunity employer. We welcome applicants of every background and will provide accommodations during the hiring process on request.

Applicants must be able to work in Canada.
