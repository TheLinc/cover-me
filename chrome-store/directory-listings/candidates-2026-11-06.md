# Free self-serve listing candidates, 2026-11-06

A research agent checked these on 2026-11-06. "Page" means the claim was verified on the site itself. "3rd" means it came from SaaSCity's or Witscode's October 2026 checks, because the site blocked fetches. Everything here can be done without emailing an editor or paying.

Ranked by expected value for AI answers:

1. SaaSHub, saashub.com/services/submit. Free with manual approval. The link is dofollow once you verify the domain (3rd). Its alternatives pages rank for resume tool searches. About 20 minutes.
2. G2 free profile, g2.com/seller_identification/search/new. The same form covers Capterra and GetApp. Verification takes 3 to 5 business days. Free profiles have no website link, and Capterra may reject a "personal app" (3rd). In one third-party test, G2 was cited in 4 of 10 ChatGPT answers and 6 of 10 Perplexity answers.
3. emredurukn/awesome-job-boards on GitHub (1.1k stars), as a PR to "Job Search Tools". It merged similar tools between Oct 30 and Nov 4, 2026 (page).
4. Show HN, news.ycombinator.com/submit. HN bans AI-written text, so the owner writes it.
5. LibHunt, libhunt.com/repo/submit. The form only asks for the repo URL (page, partial).
6. Peerlist Launchpad (weekly, nofollow), Microlaunch (microlaunch.net/submit) and Crunchbase (free, nofollow).
7. Lower value: lukasz-madon/awesome-remote-job (50k stars, written for job boards, may reject), SourceForge GitHub importer, Crozdesk, AI Valley, PeerPush (long free queue).

Free only with a badge or backlink: Dang.ai and Fazier. Uneed sets the launch date at least 50 days out and needs 10 upvotes to stay listed.

Paid now, so skip: There's An AI For That ($49), Toolify ($99), Futurepedia ($497), TopAI.tools ($47), OpenTools, Stork.ai ($49). AI Tool Hunt is offline, and sindresorhus/awesome-chatgpt has paused submissions.
